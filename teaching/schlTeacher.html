Shape the future by nurturing young minds. A school teacher is the backbone of society — inspiring curiosity, building character, and laying the foundation of every successful career.

📚

Who is a School Teacher?

A school teacher educates students from Class 1 to 12 in subjects like Mathematics, Science, English, Social Studies, etc. They plan lessons, evaluate students, and create a positive learning environment. Teachers work in government or private schools and play a vital role in holistic child development.

Salary Overview

Primary Teacher ₹25K–40K Govt. school, job security
TGT (Trained) ₹35K–55K Classes 6–10
PGT (Post Grad) ₹45K–70K Classes 11–12
Private School ₹20K–60K Varies by school

Educational Pathways

10+2 Any Stream Minimum qualification
D.El.Ed Diploma in Ed. Primary teaching (2 yrs)
B.Ed Bachelor of Ed. Secondary teaching (2 yrs)
CTET/TET Eligibility Test Mandatory for govt. jobs

Top Entrance Exams

  • CTET — Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CBSE schools)
  • State TET — State-wise teacher eligibility tests
  • KVS / NVS — Kendriya / Navodaya Vidyalaya recruitment
  • DSSSB — Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board
  • SUPER TET — Uttar Pradesh teacher recruitment

Career Opportunities

  • Government School Teacher (Primary / TGT / PGT)
  • Private School Teacher (CBSE / ICSE / IB schools)
  • Special Education Teacher
  • School Counselor / Coordinator
  • Vice Principal / Principal (with experience)
  • Coaching Institute Instructor
  • Content Writer for Educational Publishers

Pros & Cons

✅ Advantages

  • Job security (especially govt. jobs)
  • Summer & festival vacations
  • Pension & govt. benefits
  • Societal respect & impact
  • Good work-life balance

⚠️ Challenges

  • Competitive entrance exams
  • Low salary in many private schools
  • Administrative workload
  • Emotionally demanding
  • Slow career progression

Key Skills Required

  • Subject matter expertise
  • Communication & presentation skills
  • Patience and empathy
  • Classroom management
  • Lesson planning & curriculum design
  • Digital literacy (smart boards, online tools)
