School Teacher

A school teacher teaches students in primary, middle, or secondary classes and helps in their academic and personal development.

Types of School Teachers

Type Classes Detail
Primary Teacher (PRT) Class 1-5 Teaches all basic subjects
Trained Graduate Teacher (TGT) Class 6-10 Teaches subjects like Math, Science, English, Social Science
Post Graduate Teacher (PGT) Class 11-12 Teaches Specialized subjects like Physics, History, Economics


Eligiblity
For Primary Teacher (PRT)
  • 12th pass
  • D.El.Ed (Diploma in Elementary Education) or B.El.Ed

Trained Graduate Teacher (TGT)
  • Bachelor’s Degree (BA/BSc/BCom)
  • B.Ed (Bachelor of Education)
  • CTET / State TET qualified

Post Graduate Teacher (PGT)
  • Master’s Degree in subject (MA/MSc/MCom)
  • B.Ed (Bachelor of Education)
  • CTET / State TET qualifie(Teacher eligibility exam may be required)
Important Teacher Eligibility Exams
  • CTET– For central government schools
  • Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan-Teachers recuirment Exam
  • Navodya Vidyalay SamitiTeachers recuirment Exam
