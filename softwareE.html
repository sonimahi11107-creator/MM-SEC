Software Engineer

A Software Engineer designs, develops, tests, and maintains computer programs, websites, mobile apps, and software systems. Software engineers work in technology companies, startups, banks, government organizations, and IT service companies.

Eligiblity/Education Path

After 12th (Science / Maths recommended) ,br>

  • Bachelor’s Degree B.Tech / B.E. in Computer Science BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications) B.Sc Computer Science

  • Optional Higher Studies- MCA (Master of Computer Applications) M.Tech in Computer Science

Entrance exams include:
  • JEE Mains
  • NIMCET
