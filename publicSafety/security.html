🔍 What is a Security Guard?

A Security Guard protects people, property, and assets from theft, vandalism, and unauthorized access. They patrol premises, monitor CCTV, control access points, and respond to security incidents. Security professionals work in malls, banks, airports, corporate offices, events, and residential complexes.

🧠 Is This Career Right for You?

  • You are alert, observant, and detail-oriented
  • You are physically fit and can stand/patrol for long hours
  • You are responsible, trustworthy, and reliable
  • You can de-escalate conflicts calmly
  • You are comfortable with shift work including nights
  • You want to grow into corporate or cyber security roles

🎓 How to Join

Requirements

  • 10th pass minimum (12th preferred)
  • Age: 18–45 years
  • Physical fitness test
  • Background verification mandatory
  • Training from PSARA licensed security agencies

Advanced Certifications

  • CCTV Operator Certification
  • Fire Safety Training
  • First Aid & Emergency Response
  • Certified Protection Officer (CPO) — for senior roles

💼 Career Roles

  • Security Guard / Watchman
  • Security Supervisor / Team Leader
  • CCTV / Control Room Operator
  • Corporate Security Manager
  • Event Security Specialist
  • VIP / Personal Security Officer (PSO)

💰 Salary Range (India)

  • Security Guard (entry): ₹1.8 – ₹3 LPA
  • Security Supervisor: ₹3 – ₹6 LPA
  • Corporate Security Manager: ₹8 – ₹20 LPA
  • VIP / PSO: ₹6 – ₹15 LPA

⚖️ Pros & Cons

👍 Pros

  • Easy entry — minimal education required
  • Massive demand — millions of jobs across India
  • Growth path into corporate security management
  • Ex-military and police get faster promotions

👎 Cons

  • Low starting salary
  • Night shifts and 12-hour duties are common
  • Physical risk in high-security environments
  • Limited career growth without further education
