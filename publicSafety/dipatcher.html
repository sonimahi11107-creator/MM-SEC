🔍 What is an Emergency Dispatcher?

An Emergency Dispatcher (also called 911/112 Operator or Emergency Call Handler) receives distress calls from the public and coordinates police, fire, and ambulance responses. They are the critical link between people in crisis and the emergency services that save them — working in control rooms 24/7.

🧠 Is This Career Right for You?

  • You stay extremely calm under pressure and in crisis situations
  • You are an excellent listener and communicator
  • You can multitask — managing calls, radio, and computer simultaneously
  • You make fast, accurate decisions with limited information
  • You are empathetic but professional with distressed callers
  • You can handle the emotional weight of emergency situations

🎓 How to Join

Requirements

  • 12th pass minimum
  • Good communication skills in Hindi and local language
  • Basic computer proficiency
  • Recruitment through state police / emergency departments
  • GVK EMRI (108), CATS Ambulance recruit dispatchers

Training Provided

  • Emergency call handling protocols
  • CAD (Computer Aided Dispatch) software
  • Medical pre-arrival instructions (EMD certified)
  • Crisis communication and de-escalation

💼 Work Settings

  • Police Control Room (PCR) — 100 helpline
  • Ambulance Dispatch Centre — 108 / 102
  • Fire Department Control Room — 101
  • National Emergency Number — 112
  • Disaster Management Operations Centres
  • Hospital Emergency Coordination Centres

💰 Salary Range (India)

  • Junior Dispatcher / Operator: ₹2 – ₹4 LPA
  • Senior Dispatcher: ₹4 – ₹8 LPA
  • Shift Supervisor / Team Lead: ₹8 – ₹14 LPA
  • Government roles include pension and allowances

⚖️ Pros & Cons

👍 Pros

  • Save lives from behind the desk — powerful impact
  • Stable government or NGO job with fixed hours
  • No physical risk — office/control room based
  • Great entry point into emergency services field

👎 Cons

  • Extremely stressful — handling life-or-death calls constantly
  • Emotional burnout from traumatic calls
  • Shift work including nights, weekends, and holidays
  • Relatively low salary for the responsibility involved
