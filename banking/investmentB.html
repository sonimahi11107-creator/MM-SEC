🔍 What is Investment Banking?

Investment bankers help companies, governments, and institutions raise capital, execute mergers & acquisitions (M&A), and navigate complex financial transactions. They act as financial advisors for IPOs, bond issuances, corporate restructuring, and strategic decisions worth millions to billions of rupees.

🧠 Is This Career Right for You?

  • Strong analytical and quantitative skills
  • Passion for finance, markets, and economics
  • Ability to work under high pressure and long hours
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills
  • Detail-oriented with strong Excel and financial modeling ability
  • Competitive mindset and hunger to achieve results

🛠 Skills Required

  • Financial modeling & valuation (DCF, LBO, M&A models)
  • Advanced Excel & PowerPoint
  • Understanding of capital markets & corporate finance
  • Accounting and financial statement analysis
  • Bloomberg Terminal & financial databases
  • Strong written and verbal communication

📚 Educational Pathways

1. Undergraduate (3–4 years)

  • B.Com / BBA in Finance or Banking
  • B.Sc in Economics or Mathematics
  • B.Tech + MBA (popular IIT-IIM route)

2. Postgraduate (Recommended)

  • MBA in Finance — IIMs, ISB, XLRI
  • CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst) — global gold standard
  • CA + MBA combo — highly valued

3. Top Institutes (India)

  • IIM Bangalore / Ahmedabad / Calcutta
  • Indian School of Business (ISB)
  • XLRI Jamshedpur, FMS Delhi

💼 Career Opportunities

  • Investment Banking Analyst / Associate at top banks
  • M&A Advisory — helping companies merge or acquire
  • Equity Capital Markets (ECM) — managing IPOs
  • Debt Capital Markets (DCM) — bond issuances
  • Private Equity / Venture Capital (exit opportunity)
  • Hedge Fund Manager (advanced career)
  • Corporate Finance at large companies (CFO track)
