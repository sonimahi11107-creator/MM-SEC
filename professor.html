Professor

Eligiblity/Qualification Required

  • Bachelor's Degree (Graduation):B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., B.Tech etc.(at least 55%–60% marks is required to continue for higher studies.)
  • Master’s Degree (Post-Graduation):M.A., M.Sc., M.Com., M.Tech etc. According to UGC rules, you need minimum 55% marks (50% for reserved categories) in your Master’s degree.
  • Qualifying the NET / SET Exam
    1. National Level
      2. UGC NET UGC NET Conducted by NTA. It allows you to become Assistant Professor and also apply for JRF (Junior Research Fellowship).
    2. State Level
      3. SET (State Eligibility Test) – Valid for teaching in colleges of that particular state. If you complete a PhD, sometimes NET may not be required for some university positions.


    PhD (Doctor of Philosophy) A PhD is usually required to become a Professor and for promotions.
    Duration: 3–6 years
    PhD admission usually requires: Entrance exams like NET-JRF, university entrance tests, or interviews.
