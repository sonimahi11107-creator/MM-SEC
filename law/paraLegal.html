🔍 What is a Paralegal?

A Paralegal (also called a Legal Assistant) supports lawyers and law firms by conducting legal research, drafting documents, organizing case files, and assisting in court preparation. They do not represent clients in court but are essential to the smooth functioning of legal practices.

🧠 Is This Career Right for You?

  • You enjoy legal work but prefer a support role
  • You are highly organized and detail-oriented
  • You have strong research and writing skills
  • You work efficiently under deadlines
  • You want to enter the legal field without a full LLB degree
  • You are a team player who supports others to succeed

🎓 Education & Skills

Education

  • Diploma in Paralegal Studies
  • BA in Legal Studies or Political Science
  • LLB degree (gives more opportunities)
  • Certificate courses in legal drafting and research

Key Skills

  • Legal research using databases (SCC Online, Manupatra)
  • Drafting legal documents and pleadings
  • Case file management and documentation
  • Court filing procedures and deadlines
  • MS Office and legal software proficiency

💼 Work Areas

  • Law firms — assisting senior advocates
  • Corporate legal departments
  • Government legal offices
  • NGOs and legal aid organizations
  • Real estate and property firms
  • Insurance and banking legal teams

💰 Salary Range (India)

  • Entry Level: ₹2 – ₹4 LPA
  • Mid-Level: ₹5 – ₹10 LPA
  • Senior Paralegal: ₹12 – ₹20 LPA
  • Corporate sector pays significantly more than law firms

⚖️ Pros & Cons

👍 Pros

  • Good entry point into the legal field
  • Stable demand in law firms and corporations
  • Learn practical legal skills on the job
  • Can pursue LLB alongside work to advance career

👎 Cons

  • Lower pay compared to lawyers
  • Limited authority — always support role
  • Can be stressful during court deadlines
  • Career growth ceiling without LLB degree
