🧭 Introduction

Animation is a creative field where you design motion and bring ideas to life using tools like Blender, Maya, and After Effects.

2D animation is used in cartoons and storytelling videos.

3D animation is used in movies, games, and realistic visuals.

VFX adds cinematic effects like explosions and CGI.

Motion graphics are used in ads and UI animations.

🛠 Skills Required

Creativity, storytelling, Blender, After Effects, patience.
