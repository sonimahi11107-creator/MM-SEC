🔍 What is a Doctor?

A Doctor (MBBS) is a licensed medical professional who diagnoses diseases, treats patients, and promotes public health. MBBS — Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery — is the foundational medical degree in India. Doctors work in hospitals, clinics, research, and public health systems.

🧠 Is This Career Right for You?

  • You have a deep desire to help and heal people
  • You are strong in Biology, Chemistry, and Science
  • You can handle emotional and physically demanding situations
  • You are patient, empathetic, and a good communicator
  • You are committed to years of education and training
  • You can make quick, accurate decisions under pressure

🎓 Education Path

Eligibility

  • 12th with Physics, Chemistry, Biology (PCB)
  • Minimum 50% marks (General category)
  • Must qualify NEET-UG exam

Degree

  • MBBS — 5.5 years (4.5 academic + 1 year internship)
  • MD / MS — 3 year postgraduate specialization
  • Super-specialization — DM / MCh (3 more years)

Top Institutes

  • AIIMS (New Delhi, Mumbai, etc.)
  • JIPMER, Puducherry
  • Maulana Azad Medical College, Delhi
  • Grant Medical College, Mumbai

💼 Career Opportunities

  • General Physician / Family Doctor
  • Specialist — Cardiologist, Neurologist, Surgeon, etc.
  • Medical Officer (Government hospitals)
  • Medical Researcher / Clinical Trials
  • Medical Professor / Academic Doctor
  • Public Health Officer / WHO / UN agencies

💰 Salary Range (India)

  • MBBS Government Doctor: ₹8 – ₹15 LPA
  • Private Hospital Doctor: ₹12 – ₹30 LPA
  • Specialist (MD/MS): ₹25 – ₹80 LPA
  • Super Specialist: ₹80 LPA – ₹2 Cr+

⚖️ Pros & Cons

👍 Pros

  • One of the most respected professions in India
  • High earning potential after specialization
  • Job security — doctors are always in demand
  • Deep personal satisfaction from saving lives

👎 Cons

  • Very long education — 10+ years to specialize
  • High stress and emotionally draining work
  • Long working hours including night shifts
  • NEET competition is extremely fierce
