MBBS(Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery)



Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery is a professional medical degree that prepares students to become doctors. It focuses on diagnosing diseases, treating patients, and improving public health.


Eligibility Criteria:
  • 12th class with Physics, Chemistry, Biology (PCB)
  • Minimum 50% marks (General category)
  • Must qualify NEET-UG
Course Duration:5.5 Years(4.5 years academic study,1 year compulsory internship in hospital.)
Career Oportunities
  • General Physician
  • Medical Officer
  • Hospital Doctor
  • Researcher
  • Medical Professor
  • Public Health Officer

Further Specialization Option:
  • MD (Doctor of Medicine)
  • MS (Master of Surgery)
  • Super-specialization (DM / MCh)
