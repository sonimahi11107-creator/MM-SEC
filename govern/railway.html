World's 4th largest railway network. A secure, prestigious government career with excellent benefits — available for all education levels.

🔍 About Railway Recruitment

Indian Railways is one of the largest employers in the world with over 13 lakh employees. Recruitment is done through Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) across India. Jobs range from Group A gazetted officers to Group D non-gazetted staff — making it accessible to candidates from 10th pass to graduates and engineers.

📋 Major Railway Exams

NTPC Non-Technical Popular Categories Station Master, Goods Guard, Clerk (Graduation required)
JE Junior Engineer Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, IT (Diploma/B.Tech)
ALP Assistant Loco Pilot Train driver track (ITI / Diploma required)
Group D Level 1 Posts Track Maintainer, Helper, Porter (10th pass)

✅ Eligibility

RRB NTPC

  • Graduation for most posts (12th for some junior posts)
  • Age: 18–33 years (relaxation for reserved categories)

RRB JE

  • B.Tech / Engineering Degree or 3-year Diploma in relevant branch
  • Age: 18–33 years

RRB Group D

  • 10th pass + ITI or 12th pass (for some posts)
  • Age: 18–33 years

📚 Exam Pattern & Preparation

CBT Stage 1 (All exams)

  • Mathematics — Number system, Algebra, Percentage, Time & Work
  • General Intelligence & Reasoning — Puzzles, Series, Coding
  • General Awareness — Current Affairs, Railways GK, Science

CBT Stage 2 (NTPC / JE)

  • Technical subjects for JE (branch-specific)
  • Advanced GS + Reasoning for NTPC

🎖️ Popular Railway Posts

  • Station Master — manages station operations
  • Goods Guard — responsible for freight train safety
  • Junior Engineer — technical infrastructure maintenance
  • Assistant Loco Pilot → Loco Pilot (train driver)
  • Ticket Collector / Commercial Clerk
  • Track Maintainer / Technician
  • IRMS Officers (via UPSC — Indian Railway Management Service)

💰 Salary & Perks

Group D ₹18,000 + DA, HRA, PF
NTPC / ALP ₹25–35K In-hand monthly
JE / Station Master ₹35–50K + Allowances
Special Perks Free Pass Travel all India free

Railway Pass — employees and family get free/concessional train travel across India — a unique and highly valued perk.

⚖️ Pros & Cons

👍 Pros

  • One of India's largest employers — many vacancies
  • Accessible from 10th pass level
  • Free railway travel pass for self + family
  • Pension, medical, housing benefits

👎 Cons

  • Shift duties — nights, weekends included
  • Postings can be in remote locations
  • Physical fitness required for some roles
  • Competition is very high (crores apply)
