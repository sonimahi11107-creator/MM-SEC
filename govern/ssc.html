🔍 What is SSC?

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) recruits staff for various posts in Ministries, Departments, and Organizations of the Government of India. It conducts multiple exams each year — the most popular being SSC CGL, SSC CHSL, SSC MTS, and SSC CPO. These are entry points to stable, respected central government careers.

📋 Major SSC Exams

CGL Combined Graduate Level Group B & C posts — Inspector, Auditor, Tax Assistant
CHSL Combined Higher Secondary LDC, DEO, Postal Assistant (12th pass)
CPO Central Police Org. SI in CISF, BSF, CRPF, ITBP
MTS Multi Tasking Staff Group C non-gazetted posts (10th pass)

✅ Eligibility

SSC CGL (Most Popular)

  • Any graduate degree from recognized university
  • Age: 18–32 years (varies by post)
  • OBC: 3 yr relaxation | SC/ST: 5 yr relaxation

SSC CHSL

  • 12th pass (any stream)
  • Age: 18–27 years

SSC MTS

  • 10th pass (Matriculation)
  • Age: 18–25 years

📚 SSC CGL Exam Pattern

  • Tier 1: General Intelligence, GK, Quantitative Aptitude, English (Objective, 60 min)
  • Tier 2: Math, English Language & Comprehension (Objective)
  • Tier 3: Descriptive Paper — Essay/Letter (for some posts)
  • Tier 4: Computer Proficiency / Skill Test (for specific posts)

Key Subjects to Study

  • Quantitative Aptitude — Math (Chapters: Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry)
  • English — Grammar, Vocabulary, Reading Comprehension
  • General Awareness — Current Affairs, History, Geography, Science
  • Reasoning — Logical, Verbal, Non-verbal

🎖️ Posts After SSC CGL

  • Income Tax Inspector — CBDT
  • Central Excise Inspector — CBIC
  • Assistant Section Officer — MEA, Ministry of Finance
  • Sub-Inspector — CBI, NIA
  • Auditor — CAG, Controller General of Accounts
  • Statistical Investigator — Ministry of Statistics
  • Junior Statistical Officer (JSO)

⚖️ Pros & Cons

👍 Pros

  • Very high number of vacancies every year
  • Accessible — open to graduates of any stream
  • Excellent job security + pension
  • Good work-life balance compared to private sector

👎 Cons

  • Very high competition — lakhs apply each year
  • Slow promotions and salary increments
  • Some posts involve transfers across India
  • Lengthy recruitment process (1–2 years)
