🔍 What is UPSC?

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducts the Civil Services Examination (CSE) — India's toughest and most respected exam. Clearing it opens doors to prestigious services like IAS (Indian Administrative Service), IPS (Indian Police Service), IFS (Indian Foreign Service), and 20+ other All India and Central Services.

📋 Exam Structure

01 Prelims 2 papers — GS + CSAT (Objective)
02 Mains 9 papers — Essay, GS, Optional (Descriptive)
03 Interview Personality Test — 275 marks

✅ Eligibility

  • Any graduate degree from a recognized university
  • Age: 21–32 years (General) | relaxation for OBC, SC/ST
  • Attempts: 6 (General) | 9 (OBC) | Unlimited (SC/ST up to age limit)
  • Indian citizen (for most services)

📚 How to Prepare

Key Subjects

  • History, Geography, Polity, Economy (NCERT foundation)
  • Current Affairs — The Hindu / Indian Express daily
  • Science & Technology, Environment & Ecology
  • Optional Subject (choose from 48 options)
  • Essay Writing and Answer Writing Practice

Top Resources

  • NCERT Books (6th–12th) — mandatory foundation
  • Laxmikanth for Polity, Bipin Chandra for History
  • Insights on India — free online resource
  • Vision IAS, Drishti IAS, Forum IAS coaching materials
  • UPSC previous year papers — essential practice

Preparation Timeline

  • Serious preparation: 12–18 months minimum
  • Average successful candidate: 2–4 attempts
  • Daily study: 8–10 hours recommended

🎖️ Services After Clearing UPSC

  • IAS — District Collector, Secretary, Chief Secretary level
  • IPS — SP, DIG, IGP, DGP level police leadership
  • IFS — Ambassador, High Commissioner (foreign postings)
  • IRS — Income Tax, Customs & Central Excise
  • IRTS, IRAS, IAAS, IDAS — Railway, Audit, Defense services

💰 Salary & Perks

IAS / IPS ₹56,100+ Starting Basic Pay
Secretary Level ₹2.5L+ Monthly CTC
Perks Bungalow Car, Staff, Security
Pension Lifetime + Medical Benefits

⚖️ Pros & Cons

👍 Pros

  • Highest prestige government job in India
  • Power to create real change in society
  • Excellent perks — housing, car, security
  • Lifetime pension and job security

👎 Cons

  • Extremely tough — only ~1000 selected per year
  • Years of dedicated preparation required
  • Frequent transfers across states
  • Political pressure in administrative roles
