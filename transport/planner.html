Transport Planner

Design the systems that move cities. Transport planners shape how people and goods travel — designing road networks, metro routes, freight corridors, and smart mobility solutions for a rapidly urbanizing India.

🗺️

Who is a Transport Planner?

A transport planner analyzes, designs, and implements transportation systems including roads, railways, airports, metro networks, and freight corridors. They work with government bodies (NHAI, MoRTH, Metro Rail corporations), consulting firms, and urban development authorities to create efficient, safe, and sustainable mobility solutions. With India's massive infrastructure push (PM GatiShakti, Smart Cities Mission), this is a high-growth field.

Pros & Cons

✅ Advantages

  • India's infrastructure boom = huge demand
  • Work on iconic, large-scale projects
  • Meaningful impact on millions of lives
  • Strong govt. & consulting opportunities
  • International project exposure

⚠️ Challenges

  • Slow-moving govt. project timelines
  • Requires multidisciplinary knowledge
  • Field work in varying conditions
  • Political & bureaucratic hurdles
  • Niche field — limited awareness

Key Skills Required

  • Traffic modeling & simulation (VISSIM, TransCAD, VISUM)
  • GIS & spatial analysis (ArcGIS, QGIS)
  • AutoCAD & infrastructure design
  • Policy analysis & urban planning knowledge
  • Data collection & statistical analysis
  • Report writing & stakeholder presentation
