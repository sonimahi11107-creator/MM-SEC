Supply Chain Analyst

Optimize the engine of global commerce. Supply chain analysts use data, process thinking, and strategic planning to make the flow of goods faster, cheaper, and more resilient — a critical role in every major industry.

🔗

Who is a Supply Chain Analyst?

A supply chain analyst examines and improves all steps involved in getting a product from raw material to the end customer — procurement, manufacturing, warehousing, and delivery. They use data and analytics to identify bottlenecks, reduce costs, improve lead times, and build resilient supply networks. They work in FMCG, pharma, automotive, retail, e-commerce, and manufacturing companies.

  • Supply Chain Analyst at FMCG / pharma / auto companies
  • Procurement Analyst / Sourcing Specialist

  • Operations Research Analyst
  • Supply Chain Manager → Director → VP

Pros & Cons

✅ Advantages

  • High demand across all industries
  • Strong analytical + strategic career
  • Excellent growth to consulting & leadership
  • Data-driven — rewarding for analytical minds
  • Global exposure in MNCs

⚠️ Challenges

  • Complex cross-functional coordination
  • Global disruptions affect supply chains
  • Requires both analytical & people skills
  • Long hours during crisis situations
  • Constant pressure to cut costs

Key Skills Required

  • Data analysis & quantitative thinking
  • ERP & SCM software proficiency
  • Process mapping & optimization
  • Negotiation & vendor management
  • Cross-functional communication
  • Understanding of global trade & Incoterms
